Proposed decision rules

  • R1 - Living in Property A. Choose living in Property A only if the household has no other housing at no cost AND counts at least 97% of avoided market rent as value (the break-even), AND can claim Prop 19 within 1 year. Basis: Model break-even about 96.8% (base case: transfer 2045, 20-year horizon).
  • R2 - Sell at transfer. Choose a sale in the transfer year if the living break-even in R1 is not met AND the household accepts the reinvestment risk shown on the dashboard. Basis: Sale ranks first in the base case when the imputed credit is below the break-even.
  • R3 - Phased entry. Choose a 24- or 36-month parking period only if the household accepts a lower expected value in a no-crash world in exchange for less entry risk in a 1929- or 2008-type decline. Basis: Crash test on the Black swan page.
  • R4 - Renting. Choose renting only if Property A appreciation is expected to exceed the break-even (about 3.4% a year), or stock returns fall below the level where renting ties a sale (about 4.9% a year). Basis: Break-evens from the model. Renting also loses Prop 19 unless the heir lives there.
  • R5 - Estate size. If the estate exceeds the federal exclusion per person, revisit all rankings with the estate-tax and liquidity plan before any sale or exchange. Basis: Federal estate tax is not in the base model.
  • R6 - Evidence gate. No path is chosen until the valuation, Property A market rent, Prop 19 eligibility and tax-rate figures are verified, or the household accepts the gap in writing. Basis: Keeps the decision tied to evidence.
  • R7 - Annual review. Rerun the model each year with updated figures, Treasury yields, CBO updates and bill status. A change that moves a break-even by 10% or more triggers a written review. Basis: Schedules the recheck.

Not legal or tax advice. Figures use placeholders for households and are rounded to the nearest $10.
