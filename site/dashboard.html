Do not treat these net-worth rankings as a recommendation. They omit personal income tax on portfolio returns and do not net income tax on rents; estate tax is not estimated because total estate assets, prior taxable gifts and portability are unknown. Debt is set to zero pending a title/lien search, and rents, insurance, maintenance and tax rates contain unverified inputs. These omissions can change the ranking. Resolve them in Questions before relying on the output.
Debt: none assumed on either property (Q10 — title report will confirm; any lien reduces every net-worth figure dollar-for-dollar). Cash flows: cumulative across both properties (rents − taxes − operating costs − maintenance − imputed rent where occupied). Taxes: income taxes and estate taxes require case-specific CPA/attorney calculations; the current comparison is not tax-complete. Liquidity / capital costs: per-path liquidity and reinvestment-risk notes under the table; exit friction is assumption-based and must be replaced with documented quotes/fees.
