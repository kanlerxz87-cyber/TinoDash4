Path comparison — same transfer date, same horizons

Debt: none assumed on either property (Q10 — title report will confirm; any lien reduces every net-worth figure dollar-for-dollar). Cash flows: cumulative across both properties (rents − taxes − operating costs − maintenance − imputed rent where occupied). Taxes: portfolio returns in SELL paths are pre-personal-income-tax (flagged simplification); rental income is taxable and not netted (flagged). Liquidity / capital costs: per-path liquidity and reinvestment-risk notes under the table; exit friction = 5.5% sell + 0.11% transfer + $110 record. Imputed rent (read this): the LIVE path's cumulative cash flow subtracts the market rent you would pay if you did not own A — a non-cash opportunity cost, not money leaving your account. That is why LIVE's cumulative cash flow can look deeply negative while its net worth stays positive: you are "paying" rent to yourself. The what-if lab's imputed-rent slider lets you set that credit to $0 (pure cash view) or to a comps-based rent.
