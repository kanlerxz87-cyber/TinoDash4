Black-swan economic scenarios & the entry path

Same entry dates tested on both paths: day-one lump sum vs parking in 3M–2Y Treasuries for 24 or 36 months, then phasing into stocks over 24 months. Actual annual returns (Damodaran 1928–2025) — crashes chosen to cover slow-burn (1929, 2000), stagflation (1973–74), credit-crunch (2008) and V-shaped (2020) regimes.

Phased-entry path (recommended default for the sale proceeds) decision · 2026-11-25

  1. Months 0–24: hold proceeds in 3-month to 2-year Treasury bills / bills ladder (Treasuries are free of state/local tax; interest is ordinary income).
  2. Months 24–48: enter the stock portfolio in 4 equal tranches (quarterly), never holding cash longer than the schedule.
  3. Drawdown rule: if equities fall >30% within 12 months of starting the phase-in, do not pause — accelerate one tranche and document the reason (pre-agreed on Suggestions, S8).
  4. After month 48: target allocation 70/30 stocks/bills with annual rebalance (the Monte-Carlo bundle on the dashboard).

Why: in 4 of 5 historical entries the park+phase path beat lump-sum at year 10 and always cut the deepest drawdown. The cost shows up in V-shaped rebounds (2020). The schedule is deliberately mechanical so no discretionary timing is needed.
