Reinvestment Risk (Shown Beside Staging Headline)

Reinvestment risk: When interest rates fall, maturing Treasuries must be reinvested at lower yields. When markets crash, phased stock purchases buy more shares — but if markets keep rising, DCA buys fewer shares. The 24-36 month Treasury buffer balances these risks.
  • Interest rate risk: Treasury yields may fall below expectations during the buffer period
  • Market timing risk: Lump-sum investing at a peak can lose 30-50% in a crash year
  • Inflation risk: Cash and short-term Treasuries may not keep pace with inflation
  • Mitigation: Ladder Treasuries (3M, 6M, 1Y, 2Y); phase stocks gradually; review annually
