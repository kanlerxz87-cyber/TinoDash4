Key Deadlines & Timeline

Shows deadlines as a timeline. Inheritance expected ~2040-2050. All dates are illustrative — confirm with your attorney and CPA.

At Death / Transfer

Date of Death / Trust Transfer

  • Property basis steps up to fair market value at date of death
  • Obtain written valuation (appraisal) of Property A at date of death
  • Notify Santa Clara County Assessor of transfer
  • File change in ownership statement (if required)
Within 1 Year of Transfer

Prop 19 — Primary Residence Election Window

  • If claiming Prop 19 exclusion: heir must move into Cupertino home as primary residence
  • File BOE-19-P (Claim for Reassessment Exclusion) with county assessor
  • File homeowner's exemption
  • Miss this window → full reassessment to market value
Within 9 Months of Death

Federal Estate Tax Return (Form 706)

  • Due 9 months after date of death (if estate exceeds filing threshold)
  • 6-month extension available (Form 4768)
  • Portability election must be made on timely-filed Form 706
Within 18 Months of Death

Alternate Valuation Date

  • Executor may elect alternate valuation date (6 months after death) if it reduces estate value
  • Affects basis step-up for heirs
Annually (Every Year)

Recurring Reviews

  • Re-run inheritance model with updated inputs
  • Review black swan scenarios
  • Check property tax bill and assessment
  • Review insurance coverage and premiums
  • Track proposed legislation (federal estate tax, CA Prop 19 changes)
  • Update investment policy statement
If Selling Property

Sale Timing Considerations

  • Model sale in same tax year as inheritance — before deciding anything else
  • Like-kind exchange (1031): 45 days to identify replacement, 180 days to complete
  • Primary residence exclusion: owned & used 2 of 5 years before sale
  • Capital gains reported on tax return for year of sale
If Estate Exceeds Exclusion

Estate Tax Payment
  • Federal estate tax due 9 months after death
  • 40% on amount above $15,000,000 exclusion (2026)
  • Plan liquidity — avoid forced property sale
