Role in Cupertino Decision

If heir lives in SF (current)

Cannot claim Prop 19 primary-residence exclusion on Cupertino home (can only have one primary residence). Cupertino would be fully reassessed if kept as rental.

If heir moves to Cupertino

SF home could remain rental (income retained) OR be sold. Cupertino could qualify for Prop 19 if heir moves in within 1 year.

If SF home is sold

Capital gains exclusion may apply if SF is primary residence (up to $250K/$500K). Proceeds could fund Cupertino costs or be reinvested.
