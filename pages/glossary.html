Glossary

Grouped menu terms and definitions used across the dashboard.

TermDefinitionSource
Step-up in basis Adjustment of the tax basis of inherited property to its fair market value at the date of death, reducing capital gains on later sale. IRS Topic No. 403
Proposition 13 California constitutional amendment capping base property tax rate at 1% of assessed value and limiting annual assessment increases to 2% while ownership is unchanged. CA State Board of Equalization
Proposition 19 California measure (effective Feb 16, 2021) narrowing parent-child property tax reassessment exclusion to primary residences only, with a $1,044,586 value cap (2026, indexed). CA Board of Equalization — Prop 19
Base year value The assessed value established when a property was purchased or transferred, which serves as the baseline for Prop 13 annual 2% increases. CA State Board of Equalization
Factored base year value The base year value adjusted annually by up to 2% for inflation under Proposition 13. CA State Board of Equalization
Tax Rate Area (TRA) A geographic area in a county with a unique combination of taxing jurisdictions; determines the combined property tax rate applied to a parcel. Santa Clara County Assessor
Step-up in basis at death IRS rule that inherited property's basis is generally its fair market value at the decedent's date of death. IRS Topic No. 403
Capital gains tax Tax on profit from sale of an asset. Federal long-term rates: 0%/15%/20%; California taxes gains as ordinary income (no preferential rate). IRS Topic No. 409
Depreciation recapture Tax on depreciation deductions previously taken on rental property, recaptured at up to 25% when the property is sold. IRS Topic No. 414
Like-kind exchange (1031) IRS Section 1031 allows deferral of capital gains by reinvesting proceeds from sale of investment property into similar property. 45-day identification, 180-day completion. IRS Topic No. 613
Portability Election allowing a surviving spouse to use a deceased spouse's unused federal estate tax exclusion. Requires filing Form 706. IRS
Primary residence exclusion Up to $250,000 ($500,000 married) of gain on sale of a primary residence excluded from capital gains, if owned and used 2 of 5 years before sale. IRS Topic No. 701
Estate tax exclusion (2026) Federal basic exclusion amount: $15,000,000 per person. Estates below this amount owe no federal estate tax. Top rate 40% on excess. IRS / Rev. Proc. 2025-32
Phased-investing Strategy of parking sale proceeds in short-term Treasuries (3M-2Y) for 24-36 months, then gradually phasing into stocks — softens crash entry risk. TinoDash4 model design
Reinvestment risk Risk that maturing investments must be reinvested at lower yields, or that dollar-cost averaging buys fewer shares in rising markets. TinoDash4 model design
Black swan Extreme, unpredictable economic event with severe impact (e.g., 1929 Depression, 2008 GFC). Modeled separately from baseline. TinoDash4 model design
Imputed rent The estimated rental value of owner-occupied housing — treated as a cost (opportunity cost of living in rather than renting out). TinoDash4 model design
Form 706 United States Estate (and Generation-Skipping Transfer) Tax Return — filed when estate exceeds filing threshold or to elect portability. IRS Form 706
Form 540 California resident income tax return. Includes 1% mental health services tax on income over $1 million — verify against current instructions. CA Franchise Tax Board
BOE-19-P California claim form for parent-child reassessment exclusion under Proposition 19. CA Board of Equalization
