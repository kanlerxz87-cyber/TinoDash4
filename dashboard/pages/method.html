Method and privacy

Privacy

  • No street address, ZIP code, parcel number or account number appears in the repository or on this site. Properties are labelled "Property A" and "Property B", with the county only.
  • People are shown as placeholders: [HEIR], [TRUST], [ATTORNEY], [CPA].
  • Every dollar figure is rounded to the nearest $10. Statutory amounts are printed exactly and are checked against the source register.
  • The owner figures published here are property values, insurance, utilities and rent ranges, and tax history. No income, account or debt figures were supplied; none appear.
  • Earlier copies of the site were removed from the published tree (F-14). Git history still contains them, and a rewrite needs owner approval (SG-10).

Model

  • Monthly simulation over 10 and 20 years for each path, with transfers in 2040 and 2050.
  • The primary metric is the real financial position at the end of the horizon. A property still held is sold at that point, with selling costs and tax. Imputed rent is excluded (F-25).
  • Monte Carlo: 2,000 paths. Each year of a path draws one year at random, with replacement, from the 1928-2025 record; equity and bill returns come from the same year. Seed 20261126.
  • Household cash flows (rent in; costs and tax out) go to the cash account at the bill return (F-24).
  • Crash scenarios replay the historical sequences from 1929, 2008, 1973 and 2000.
  • Rules: applied mechanically from DECISION_RULES.md. The model does not set them.
  • Property B is in every path and excluded from the comparison.

Limits

  • Owner value estimates stand in for appraisals.
  • No mortgage or other debt is modelled (Q-09).
  • Only financial returns are simulated; home values are not.
  • Annual return data are applied as monthly compounding; a fall inside one year is not captured.
  • Income tax uses one combined rate; the household's brackets are unknown (Q-08).

Reproduce

python3 tools/model.py then python3 tools/build_site.py then python3 tools/check_site.py. The model reads only files in this repository.
