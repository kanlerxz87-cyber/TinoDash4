Paths compared

Eight paths for Property A, compared at the same transfer years (2040 and 2050) and horizons (10 and 20 years). Values are the financial position at the end of the horizon, in real dollars (deflated by the 2.3% inflation assumption), rounded to $10. A property still held at the end is valued as if sold then, after selling costs and tax, so the paths are comparable. Property B is held in every path and is left out. Median and percentiles come from 2,000 simulated paths (seed 20261126); crash columns replay the historical sequence from that year.

Imputed rent (the rent a living owner does not pay) is not cash, so it is excluded from the financial position used by the rules and shown separately. Mixed and phased paths are listed beside the pure choices.

How to read the columns

  • Bills 1% from year 4: the same path if T-bill yields fall to 1% from year 4. It shows how much a path depends on the rate earned while cash waits.
  • Taxes: property tax on the Proposition 19 or reassessed value, capital-gain tax at sale (basis is the value at death), depreciation recapture for rented paths, and tax on net rental income.
  • Rule status: the rules are on the rules page.
