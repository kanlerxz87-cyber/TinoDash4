Glossary

Plain-language definitions. Sources are in the Sources register.

Assessed value
The value on which property tax is computed. Under Proposition 13 it rises by at most the lesser of inflation or 2% a year, until a change of ownership.
Basis (step-up)
The income-tax cost of an inherited asset: its fair market value on the date of death (IRS Pub 551).
Basic exclusion amount
The federal estate-tax exemption per decedent: $15,000,000 for 2026.
Behavioral Health Services Tax
California surtax of 1% on taxable income above $1,000,000 (FTB Form 540-ES worksheet).
Documentary transfer tax
Tax on a recorded sale: $1.10 per $1,000 at county level (statute snippet; city rates vary, F-10).
Imputed rent
The rent an owner saves by living in the home. Not cash; reported separately.
Median (real)
The middle outcome of the simulated paths after inflation. Half are above, half below.
Mixed path
A path that changes mode, for example living for three years and then selling or renting.
Phasing
Moving sale proceeds from cash into equities in equal tranches, instead of all at once.
Proposition 13 / Proposition 19
Proposition 13 limits annual growth of assessed value. Proposition 19 limits the parent-child exclusion to a family home the child occupies.
Reinvestment risk
The risk that cash waiting to be invested earns less, or is invested at worse prices, than expected.
Section 121 exclusion
Gain exclusion on a main home: $250,000 single, $500,000 joint, with ownership and use tests (IRS Pub 523).
Tax-rate area
A county geographic area with its own total rate (levy plus voter-approved bonds).
Unrecaptured section 1250 gain
Depreciation taken on rental property, taxed at up to 25% federal when the property is sold.
