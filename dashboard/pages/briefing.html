Pick under the written rules: Sell, lump sum, median $20,199,660. Phased (24 months): median $16,211,010; 1929-type crash $9,683,850, against $4,626,110 for the lump sum.

For the attorney

  1. Trust terms: is A transferred by deed, and does the Proposition 19 parent-child rule apply?
  2. Will the heir occupy A within a year? A written commitment is needed before any living path can be chosen.
  3. Portability: is there a surviving spouse, and are there prior taxable gifts? Should a timely Form 706 be filed?
  4. Is a date-of-death appraisal in hand, and who prepares it?

For the CPA

  1. Basis: step-up to date-of-death value for A and B; confirm the appraisal split of land and building.
  2. Section 121: applies only with the use and ownership tests; confirm the heir's facts.
  3. Rental B: depreciation, recapture at up to 25%, and the 3.8% net investment income tax.
  4. California: ordinary tax on gains (confirm), and the 1% surtax above $1,000,000 (confirm the top rate).
  5. Confirm the Proposition 19 assessed-value formula used in the model (F-20).
