Rules and pick

The choice rules are those written in the repository file DECISION_RULES.md on 25 November 2026, before any model output was generated. The model applies them mechanically and does not set them. A change must be dated, justified and logged in the decision log.

Rules

#Rule (as written)Status for this household
Decision item (no figure) 1Primary metric: median real (CPI-deflated) net position at the horizon, across the scenario set and the Monte Carlo draws.Applied to every path.
Decision item (no figure) 2Downside guard: a path must keep its 10th-percentile real outcome within 10% of Path C's 10th percentile. Otherwise Path C is chosen.Applied to the phased paths.
Decision item (no figure) 3Live (A or F) is eligible only if the household commits in writing to occupy A as its principal residence within 12 months. If that commitment is absent, A and F are removed from the choice set.No commitment on file: living paths removed.
Decision item (no figure) 4Rent (B) is eligible only if at least three verified rental comparables for A exist. Until then, B is reported as a floor, not a choice.None on file: rent paths removed; shown as a floor.
Decision item (no figure) 5Phased (D) beats lump sum (C) only if D's worse black-swan outcome (1929-type or 2008-type) is at least C's worse black-swan outcome, and D's base median is within 5% of C's.Applied to the phased paths.
Decision item (no figure) 6Mixed (E) is chosen only if it beats the best pure path on the primary metric and on the downside guard.No mixed path eligible.
Decision item (no figure) 7Estate tax: no federal estate-tax liability is assumed unless the total taxable estate is verified above the federal basic exclusion amount in the year of death. Other estate assets are unknown, so every result is conditional on that.Other estate assets unknown; every result is conditional.
Decision item (no figure) 8Ties: if two paths are within 2% on the primary metric, prefer the path with higher liquidity at transfer, then the lower regulatory-change exposure.Applied to the phased paths.
