Rules and pick

The choice rules are those written in the repository file DECISION_RULES.md on 25 November 2026, before any model output was generated. The model applies them mechanically and does not set them. A change must be dated, justified and logged in the decision log.

Rules

#Rule (as written)Status for this household
Internal decision item, not a sourced figure 1Primary metric: median real net position at the horizon, across the scenarios and simulated paths.Applied to every path.
Internal decision item, not a sourced figure 2Downside guard: a path's 10th-percentile real outcome must stay within 10% of the lump-sum sale's 10th percentile. If not, the lump-sum sale is chosen.Applied to the phased paths.
Internal decision item, not a sourced figure 3Living is eligible only if the household commits in writing to occupy A as its principal residence within 12 months.No commitment on file: living paths removed.
Internal decision item, not a sourced figure 4Renting is eligible only if at least three verified rental comparables for A exist. Until then, renting is reported as a floor.None on file: rent paths removed; shown as a floor.
Internal decision item, not a sourced figure 5Phased sale beats the lump sum only if its worse black-swan outcome is at least as good as the lump sum's, and its base median is within 5% of the lump sum's.Applied to the phased paths.
Internal decision item, not a sourced figure 6A mixed path is chosen only if it beats the best pure path on the primary metric and on the downside guard.No mixed path eligible.
