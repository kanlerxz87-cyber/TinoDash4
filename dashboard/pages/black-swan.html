Black-swan scenarios and economic baseline

Separate from the baseline. Baseline figures are official projections where marked. Stress scenarios replay real sequences of bad years from the 1928-2025 record. Results are for the 2050 transfer and a 20-year horizon, real, rounded to $10.

Crash scenarios replay the historical annual returns from 1929, 2008, 1973 and 2000, continuing into later years (the record wraps from 2025 to 1928). They use annual steps, so a fall inside one year is not captured month by month.

Limits

  • No historical CPI series was used. The 1973-type scenario uses that period's nominal returns and the flat 2.3% inflation, not a stagflation price path.
  • Home values are not drawn at random; only financial returns are simulated.
  • The record covers one country and one century. It has no comparable multi-decade real loss, and no month-by-month crash data.
