Inheritance steps and timeline

Step by step

  1. Date of death. Record the date. The income-tax basis of Property A becomes its fair market value that day (S07).
  2. Valuation. Obtain written appraisals of A and B at the date of death. The estimates used here are owner estimates (F-06).
  3. Trust administration. The trustee takes control of the property. The attorney confirms the trust terms and whether the transfer is by trust or by deed.
  4. Estate-tax check. Compare the gross estate and adjusted taxable gifts with $15,000,000. If above, or if a portability election is wanted, a Form 706 is needed on the due date (F-17).
  5. Property-tax claim. If the heir will live in A, file the claim and move in within the window in the BOE guidance (S03). Otherwise expect reassessment at market value (S10).
  6. Insurance. Change the policy on A to the right occupancy (owner-occupied, tenant or vacant) before the transfer date.
  7. Decision. Apply the rules. If a sale is chosen, the CPA and attorney review the holding structure first.
  8. Investing. Put the investing policy in place before the money arrives (see phased investing).
