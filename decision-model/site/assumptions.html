Assumptions & ranges

Every knob in the model, what it is set to, why, and what happens if it is wrong.

Assumptions are the model

A projection is an assumption set with arithmetic attached. This page lists every assumption, its value, where it came from, and — most importantly — what happens if it is wrong. Anything the household cannot trace to a real number is here, in the open.

The rule applied throughout

Where a value could not be established, it was not replaced with a plausible-looking number. It was replaced with a range, and the range was carried through the model as an adversarial pair of cases. That is why every headline on this site has a low and a high rather than a single figure with a false decimal point.

Round-number assumptions replaced with ranges or real figures

The household specifically objected to round-number assumptions. Every one that was in the first draft of this model has been replaced. The replacements are listed here so the change is auditable.
