The three things this table makes obvious

Property tax is the whole ballgame

Under Prop 19 the assessed value grows at 2% a year while the market value grows at 3.5%. That gap, compounded for 30 years, is the hold path's advantage. Break the occupancy condition and the assessed value resets to market, and the tax line roughly triples overnight.

Maintenance is the number the household has wrong

The household's instinct that the Property A needs less maintenance than the San Francisco one is reasonable. But “less than the other house” is not a number. At 2% of building value, annual maintenance passes $30,000 well before the horizon — and that is a smooth average that hides the $60,000 roof.

The imputed rent is doing a lot of work

Crediting the household with the rent it does not pay is the honest treatment, but it is also the input that most favours the hold path. If you think the household would live somewhere cheaper than this house, the credit is too generous. It is a first-class item on the assumptions page for exactly that reason.
