Private estate inventory

The structure to fill in offline, with every field named and no field filled. Nothing here is submitted anywhere.

A private inventory, kept private

The household asked for a private estate inventory. It cannot live on this site, because this site is public and indexed. It lives here as a template: the structure to fill in on paper or in a local file, with every field named, and no field filled.

Do not fill this in on this website

There is no form here, no input, and no submission — deliberately. Type the real values into an untracked local file (private/inventory.local.md, which .gitignore blocks) or onto paper. If the inventory ever needs to be shared, share the completed local file directly with the adviser, not a link to this site.

See Method & privacy for why this repository is public and what that means.

Why this structure and not a spreadsheet of everything

It is organised by who needs it

An attorney does not need the water bills. A CPA does not need the leases. A household meeting that tries to cover all nine sections at once covers none of them well. The sections map onto three separate meetings.

It records the why, not just the what

Every entry says what decision it informs. Two years from now, the reason for keeping a contractor invoice from 2019 will not be obvious; the reason for keeping it is basis.

It is deliberately incomplete

A template with sample values invites copying the sample. An empty template forces a real answer, and makes it obvious which entries are still missing.

This is a decision-support model, not legal, tax or investment advice. Every figure traces to a verified authority or an explicit assumption. Unverified items are on Flagged for review. Confirm everything with a California estate attorney, a CPA, and a fee-only fiduciary adviser before acting.
