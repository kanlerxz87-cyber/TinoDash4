Questions and gaps

Questions and information needed before a decision. These are not values; they have no source to link. Household answers are kept internally.

Questions and information gaps

  • Answer needed: Who is the heir, and will they live in Property A within one year of death?
    Why it matters: Decides Prop 19 eligibility, the largest single factor.. How to get it: Household decision, with trust counsel..
  • Answer needed: What is the estate's total value (all assets and debts), and is it above the federal exclusion per person?
    Why it matters: Decides whether federal estate tax and Form 706 apply.. How to get it: Inventory of all accounts, property and debts; CPA review..
  • Answer needed: Was the decedent married, and was portability elected on a prior return?
    Why it matters: Portability can add a second exclusion.. How to get it: Prior Form 706 filings; counsel..
  • Answer needed: Are there prior taxable gifts (Form 709), and what are their amounts?
    Why it matters: Prior gifts reduce the lifetime exclusion.. How to get it: Prior Form 709 returns; CPA..
  • Answer needed: Written date-of-death values for Property A and Property B.
    Why it matters: Basis (step-up) and Prop 19 values.. How to get it: Certified appraisal; counsel to confirm an acceptable appraiser..
  • Answer needed: The tax rate area and published rate for Property A's parcel.
    Why it matters: Replaces the derived rate.. How to get it: County assessor lookup (internal, with the parcel number)..
  • Answer needed: Market rent for Property A (at least three comparable rentals).
    Why it matters: Ranks the rent path on evidence, not a voucher benchmark.. How to get it: Licensed broker or appraiser rental opinion..
  • Answer needed: Insurance quotes: primary residence (Property A) and landlord policy (Property B).
    Why it matters: Replaces the unverified insurance guesses.. How to get it: Three quotes each; CDI consumer resources..
  • Answer needed: Leases, rent-control status and tenant rules for each unit in Property B.
    Why it matters: Limits on rent increases and owner move-in.. How to get it: Rent Board records; leases; counsel..
  • Answer needed: Which Measure H tier applies to the Property B building?
    Why it matters: Sets the parcel tax amount.. How to get it: Measure H text and the assessor's building record..
  • Answer needed: Interconnection date and tariff for the Property A solar system.
    Why it matters: Solar credit may be small under NEM 3.0.. How to get it: Utility account and interconnection letter..
  • Answer needed: Repair and maintenance spending over the last five years.
    Why it matters: Replaces the maintenance assumption.. How to get it: Five-year bills and receipts..
  • Answer needed: Household income in the sale year and the years after.
    Why it matters: Sets the tax rates used in every path.. How to get it: CPA projection..
  • Answer needed: Does the heir already have housing at no cost (for example an unused unit in Property B)?
    Why it matters: Sets the imputed-rent credit, the biggest swing factor.. How to get it: Household decision..
  • Answer needed: Risk tolerance: the largest acceptable loss in a 1929-type or 2008-type decline.
    Why it matters: Chooses between a lump sum and phased entry.. How to get it: Household decision, written before results..
  • Information missing: Official tax bills for both parcels.
    Why it matters: Confirms the effective rate.. How to get it: County tax bills (internal)..
  • Information missing: Official insurance premiums.
    Why it matters: Confirms the cost assumptions.. How to get it: Carrier quotes..
  • Information missing: Certified Measure H results and text.
    Why it matters: Confirms the parcel tax.. How to get it: SF Department of Elections, after certification..
  • Information missing: Bill numbers and texts for the reported federal proposals.
    Why it matters: Confirms what is proposed.. How to get it: Congress.gov search by sponsor..
  • Information missing: IRS release for the 2027 inflation adjustments; FTB 2026 rate schedule.
    Why it matters: Confirms rates used in the model.. How to get it: IRS and FTB websites (links on the Sources page)..
