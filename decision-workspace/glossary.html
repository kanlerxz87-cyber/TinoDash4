Glossary and page index

The vocabulary on this site is deliberately narrow: if a word appears in a table, it appears here. Terms are grouped by which of the three tax systems they belong to, because most confusion in this decision comes from using one system's vocabulary in another's argument.

Property tax (the assessment)

Factored base year value (FBYV)
A parcel's Prop 13 base, grown each year by CPI or 2%, whichever is lower. This - not market value - drives the bill on an unchanged property.
Ad valorem
'According to value' - a tax computed as a percentage of assessed value, as opposed to a flat parcel charge.
Non-ad-valorem / direct assessment
A flat parcel charge (water district, flood control, some bonds) billed on the tax bill regardless of value. The likeliest explanation of both properties' odd implied rates.
Tax Rate Area (TRA)
The parcel-level map code that determines which voter-approved bonds add on top of the 1% levy. Santa Clara County has 816 of them.
Change in ownership
The property-tax event that resets a base year value. A distribution under a trust can be one; whether it is depends on who holds what before and after.
Supplemental bill
A catch-up bill issued after a reassessment, for the part-year already billed at the old value. Two of them typically follow a change in ownership.
Homeowners' exemption
definition pending - this term is used on the flagged and sources pages, and will be defined here when its definition is settled
Escrow holdback / concessions
Seller-paid credits at closing. Modelled as a percentage of price so the number survives re-pricing.

Income tax (the gain)

Adjusted basis
What you 'paid' for tax purposes: stepped-up value at death, plus capital improvements, minus depreciation claimed. Gain = net sale price - adjusted basis.
Recapture (unrecaptured 1250)
Depreciation you took is taxed again on sale at up to 25% - a rate that exists specifically because the underlying gain would otherwise be taxed at 20%.
Depreciation
definition pending - this term is used on the flagged and sources pages, and will be defined here when its definition is settled
Section 121 exclusion
$250,000 (single) of gain on a principal residence, needing 2 of the last 5 years of both ownership and use. Inherited houses usually fail the 'use' test until someone moves in.
Suspended loss
A passive rental loss the tax law will not let you deduct now; it waits, unused, until there is passive income or a sale.
NIIT
Net investment income tax - 3.8% on investment income including capital gains above modified AGI thresholds. It stacks on top of the 20% rate, not instead of it.
Mid-month convention
Residential depreciation starts (and stops) as if in the middle of the month, so a December purchase gets half a month of it.

Estate / transfer (the death)

Basic exclusion amount
The federal estate/gift threshold above which the 40% estate tax applies ($15,000,000 for a 2026 death).
DSUE / portability
A deceased spouse's unused estate-tax exclusion, transferable to the surviving spouse - but only if a Form 706 was filed to elect it.
Alternate valuation date
An estate-tax election to value assets 6 months after death instead of at death. It lowers the estate tax only if values fell - and it lowers the heir's basis by the same amount.
Documentary transfer tax
definition pending - this term is used on the flagged and sources pages, and will be defined here when its definition is settled

Renting and tenancy

Owner move-in (OMI)
A San Francisco no-fault eviction allowing an owner with 25%+ ownership to take one unit for their own use, subject to a 36-month occupancy promise and relocation payments.
Just cause
definition pending - this term is used on the flagged and sources pages, and will be defined here when its definition is settled
Rent cap
definition pending - this term is used on the flagged and sources pages, and will be defined here when its definition is settled
Vacancy / turn allowance
A haircut on gross rent for empty periods and re-letting costs; 5% is used in this model as an assumption, not a measurement.

Money and method

Real vs nominal
Nominal is dollars; real is dollars divided by prices. Two properties and a stock portfolio are only comparable in real terms.
Present value
What a future dollar is worth today at a chosen discount rate. Used here to make 30-year streams comparable; the discount rate itself is an assumption on the what-if page.
Sequence-of-returns risk
The same average return producing very different outcomes depending on the ORDER of the years. It is the whole reason a sale at 2045 might be staged.
Block bootstrap
Resampling in fixed-length contiguous blocks instead of single years, so a simulated path keeps real sequences of bad years together. Used here with 3-year blocks.
Imputed rent
The market rent an owner-occupier avoids by living in their own house. It is the price of occupying the asset, so a 'live there' path is not free.
Use value
The benefit of consuming the housing yourself, as opposed to the money it could earn. Reported separately here, and never used silently.
Opportunity cost
definition pending - this term is used on the flagged and sources pages, and will be defined here when its definition is settled
Liquidity floor
definition pending - this term is used on the flagged and sources pages, and will be defined here when its definition is settled
Treasury parking lane
Holding sale proceeds in 3-month to 2-year Treasuries for a fixed period before buying risk assets, so a crash just after the sale cannot hit the whole stake at once.

Also used

BOE-19-P
The claim form that actually starts the Proposition 19 parent-child exclusion. Filing the homeowners' exemption is NOT the same filing.
RPTT
San Francisco's real property transfer tax, payable at recording, with tiered cliff rates.
