Comparables protocol - the missing evidence

Several of the twelve paths rank on a rent figure that came from the household rather than from the market. This page is the protocol for fixing that: what to collect, where it comes from, and the arithmetic it feeds. The table below is empty on purpose - the cells are blank because the evidence has not been gathered, and an illustrative comp would be read as a finding.

The comparison is made against the after-tax yield on the sale proceeds, not against 'what a house might do someday'. A rental that cannot beat bills plus a risk premium is not an investment, it is an occupation with paperwork - which may be exactly what the household wants, and is a perfectly good reason to choose a path. It is not a reason to call the path profitable.
