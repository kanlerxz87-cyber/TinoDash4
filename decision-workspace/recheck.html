The annual re-run

  1. Replace the previous calendar year's equity and bill returns in model/history.py with the published table, and note the date that table was updated. The Black-Swan and phased pages are computed from it, so a stale file silently becomes a stale conclusion.
  2. Re-read the assumption ranges in model/inputs.py against the current year: inflation, short rates, insurance, and the two house values. Change the ranges, not the point estimates - there are no point estimates.
  3. Run python3 model/build.py && python3 model/site.py and read the diff of the generated tables. A ranking that has not moved at all in twelve months is usually a sign the inputs were not touched, not that the world was stable.
  4. Re-run the two extreme scenarios specifically: entering the market in a crash year, and inflation running above the 2% assessment cap. If either changes the recommendation, write that on the decision-rules page - it is exactly the kind of fact the household agreed to collect.
  5. Move the status column on the watch page only with an effective date attached: 'proposed' does not become 'enacted' because a measure is popular.
  6. Update the decision log with one line, even if the line is 'no change, and here is why not'.
