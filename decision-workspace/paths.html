The twelve paths, compared

Twelve paths, all evaluated at the same transfer year (2045) and the same horizons (10, 20 and 30 years), including taxes, debt, occupancy, capital costs and liquidity. Pure strategies and mixed strategies sit in the same table, because the mixed ones are where the real answer lives.

PathCost of that choiceSource
