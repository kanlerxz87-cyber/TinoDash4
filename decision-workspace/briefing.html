Attorney & CPA briefing sheet - 2026-11-25

Situation

  • The client owns Property B outright: a three-unit building in San Francisco - one unit vacant and livable, two units let. The client can occupy the vacant unit without displacing a tenant.
  • The client is a beneficiary of a trust holding Property A: a single-family house in Santa Clara County with solar, expected to pass at the settlor's death, provisionally 2040-2050 (modelled at 2045).
  • The inheritance is expected to include a considerable amount of cash, possibly above the federal exclusion. The character of that cash - taxable brokerage, retirement accounts, insurance - is unknown and changes the plan more than the house does.
  • No mortgage was reported on either building. No co-beneficiary has been identified, which is itself an open question.
  • The decision sought: live in Property A, rent it, sell it, or sequence those.

Questions for counsel

  1. Q-01 Will the heir actually occupy Property A as a principal residence within one year of the transfer, and will they file for the homeowners' exemption in that window?
  2. Q-05 If the heir later wants Property B's rented units for family use or a sale with vacant possession, is an owner-move-in eviction even available on this building?
  3. Q-08 Does the trust distribute Property A outright, or hold it in a continuing trust? Are there remaindermen, no-contest terms, or a power to appoint?
  4. Q-10 Was a portability election made at the first spouse's death (Form 706 Part 6), and is there a DSUE?
  5. Q-11 Confirm with counsel that California imposes no estate or inheritance tax at the decedent's death in the modelled year.
  6. Q-14 Is a like-kind exchange wanted on either sale, and does the heir have investment (not personal) intent for the replacement property?
  7. Q-17 For Property B: does the new Muni parcel tax limit what can be passed to the two sitting tenants, and are those tenancies covered by the Rent Ordinance?
  8. Q-20 Any federal tax lien, Medi-Cal estate claim, or judgment that would attach before distribution?

Questions for the CPA

  1. Confirm in writing that California imposes no estate or inheritance tax on this transfer, with the authority relied on.
  2. Confirm the passive-activity position: are rental losses deductible against salary, suspended, or is a real-estate-professional election in play?
  3. Confirm the treatment of the 1% mental/behavioral health surtax on a large gain in a sale year, and whether the $1,000,000 threshold is indexed in the return year.
  4. Advise on section 121 where the heir occupies for about three years and then rents: the non-qualified-use proration is the one rule here that is genuinely arguable.
  5. Confirm that the trust distribution itself triggers no documentary transfer tax, and get the exemption cited rather than assumed.
  6. Produce a land/building allocation, ideally a cost-segregation study, so depreciation and recapture are not 75% of a guess.
  7. Say, before any listing, whether a 1031 exchange is worth structuring - it must be set up before closing and cannot be elected on the return.
  8. Tell us the character of the inherited cash, because an inherited retirement account has no step-up and pays ordinary income tax while a brokerage account has both.

What is already decided, and what is not

The criteria for choosing each path are written down (decision-rules page). The client has agreed not to choose a path from a single projection, to treat occupancy as the controlling fact, and to re-run annually. Counsel's view on the trust mechanics, the claim window, and any Medi-Cal or lien exposure is needed before the sell-versus-hold question is argued at all.
