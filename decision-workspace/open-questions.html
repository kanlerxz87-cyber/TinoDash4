Q-01Prop 19 eligibilityWill the heir actually occupy Property A as a principal residence within one year of the transfer, and will they file for the homeowners' exemption in that window?Without both, there is no parent-child exclusion at all and the full market value becomes the new taxable value - that is the single biggest swing in the model.Trustee + CPA; county assessor claim BOE-19-PBOE-P19 official
Q-05San Francisco eviction exposureIf the heir later wants Property B's rented units for family use or a sale with vacant possession, is an owner-move-in eviction even available on this building?Only one owner-move-in per building is allowed, the unit is then fixed forever as the 'owner's unit', tenants 60+ with 10 years or a catastrophically ill tenant with 5 years cannot be moved, and relocation costs are ~$7,910 per tenant plus $5,280 per protected person. Confirm whether the small-building limits changed after the published page.Rent Ordinance counsel; sf.gov page is the published ruleSF-OMI official
Q-08Trust mechanicsDoes the trust distribute Property A outright, or hold it in a continuing trust? Are there remaindermen, no-contest terms, or a power to appoint?A distribution to the beneficiary is the change in ownership that starts the Prop 19 1-year clock; a continuing trust can delay it and can also forfeit the exclusion.Estate attorney; read the instrument, not the summaryRTC-61 official
Q-10PortabilityWas a portability election made at the first spouse's death (Form 706 Part 6), and is there a DSUE?A DSUE can double the shelter for the surviving spouse. It is only available if a 706 was filed on time (or within the 5-year late relief window for non-filing estates).CPA / prior Form 706IRS-ESTATE official
Q-11California estate taxConfirm with counsel that California imposes no estate or inheritance tax at the decedent's death in the modelled year.This model assumes 'no CA estate tax'. It is a load-bearing assumption and was NOT confirmed against an official source in this session - it is on the flagged list.CPA; FTBno source located
Q-141031 exchange intentIs a like-kind exchange wanted on either sale, and does the heir have investment (not personal) intent for the replacement property?A residence the heir moves into cannot be the replacement property in a 1031 exchange. Deciding this after the sale is decided is usually too late - the identification windows are 45 and 180 days.CPA + qualified intermediary, before listingIRS-544 official
Q-17Prop H pass-throughFor Property B: does the new Muni parcel tax limit what can be passed to the two sitting tenants, and are those tenancies covered by the Rent Ordinance?The measure text allows a limited pass-through for rent-controlled units. Getting this wrong in either direction changes net operating income and creates Rent Board exposure.Rent counsel; Rent Board petition if neededSF-PROP-H ballot text
Q-20Creditor, tax-lien and benefit timingAny federal tax lien, Medi-Cal estate claim, or judgment that would attach before distribution?A Medi-Cal estate claim against the house can consume the equity the whole plan is built on. It must be known before the model, not after.Estate attorney; state recovery unitno source located

What we searched and did not find

  • The city's water and sewer rate schedule. Every attempt returned a different city's rates. Left blank rather than borrowed - see F-5.
  • An official edition of San Francisco Form 579 covering notices served after 2025-02-28. The latest table found is the 2024-25 one; its amounts are used and flagged as possibly superseded (F-4).
  • A Santa Clara County tax rate book for FY 2026-27. Only FY 2025-26 exists, so every rate on this site carries that label rather than a newer one.
  • The text of R&T Code 11930, the gift and death exemption from documentary transfer tax. The county materials cite it; the section itself was not read (F-10).
  • Any authority for a small-landlord exemption from eviction limits in San Francisco. The material found describes Los Angeles, so it is not assumed here.
  • The specific tax rate area for Property A. The county publishes 816 of them and the parcel-level lookup needs the assessor record, which is deliberately not stored in this repository (Q-04).
