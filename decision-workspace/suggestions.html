Method suggestions - review board

A review board, not a to-do list. Each proposal says what it costs to skip. Pick the lane at the bottom before any of it is implemented - the lanes are different amounts of work with different amounts of confidence, and that choice belongs to the household, not to the model.

The lanes are not exclusive. The order is the decision, and the honest recommendation from a model that keeps finding its own inputs missing is: Lane 2, then Lane 1, then Lane 3 - and treat Lane 4 as optional forever.
