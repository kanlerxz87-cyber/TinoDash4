What if - the sensitivity grid

Six dials. Every combination was computed by the model before this page was written, so the controls below are lookups into results, not a JavaScript reimplementation of the model. The grid is 3 × 3 × 3 × 2 × 3 = 162 published scenarios; if your view is between the dials, the nearest one is what you see.
