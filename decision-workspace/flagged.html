Deliberately left out

  • A mortgage or line of credit - none was reported on either building; if one exists, tell the model, and the ranking will move toward liquidity.
  • Rent benchmarked to voucher schedules - it would have produced a number, and a number that is not what a market tenant pays is worse than a blank cell.
  • A small-landlord exemption from eviction limits - no authority for it in San Francisco was found; the material describing it is about a different city.
  • Estate-tax planning beyond whether a return is due - the gross estate is unknown until the cash is characterised; planning on top of an unknown is fortune-telling.
  • A state inheritance tax - California is assumed to have none, and that assumption is flagged (F-6) rather than modelled as fact.
  • Names, addresses, parcel numbers and account numbers - they belong in the offline folder. This site is built so that it can be public without being legible about anyone.
