Proposed, not enacted - deliberately modelled as nothing

  • Any change to the federal step-up in basis. Repeatedly proposed, never enacted. If it ever passes with a grandfather date, the value of selling at the death jumps - so it is worth one look each January.
  • Any measure to restore the full Prop 13 parent-child transfer without the value cap or the occupancy condition. It would make the live paths and the rent paths nearly identical; it has failed at the ballot more than once.
  • Any San Francisco measure to widen the transfer-tax exemptions or to add a tier. It changes the net proceeds of a Property B sale by tens of thousands.
  • Any change to the statewide rent cap or its stated expiry around 2030. It is the difference between 'rent grows with the market' and 'rent grows with the cap' in the rent paths.

No bill numbers are quoted, because no bill was verified in this pass. A list of bill numbers copied from a search result would look like research and be a guess wearing a citation; the honest entry is 'watch these four things, and here is the authority that would tell us'.
