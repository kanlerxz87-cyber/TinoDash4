Glossary

Terms

Assessed value
The value property tax is calculated on. Under Prop 13 it rises by at most 2% a year until a change in ownership.
Basis (step-up)
The tax cost of inherited property, generally fair market value at date of death (IRC 1014).
Behavioral Health Services Tax
The 1% California surcharge on taxable income above $1,000,000 (formerly the Mental Health Services Tax).
BOE-19-P
Claim form for the Prop 19 parent-child exclusion, filed with the county assessor.
Break-even
The input value at which two paths give the same result. It shows what would change the answer.
DSUE (deceased spouse's unused exclusion)
Amount a surviving spouse may add to his or her own exclusion through a portability election.
Form 706
Federal estate tax return. Also used to elect portability.
Form 709
Federal gift tax return.
Imputed rent
The value of living in a home you own, measured as the rent you would otherwise pay.
Like-kind exchange (section 1031)
Deferral of gain on investment real estate exchanged for other investment real estate.
Mixed path
A plan that combines choices over time, such as living for two years and then selling.
Monte Carlo distribution
Many simulated outcomes from random draws, used to show a range of results rather than one number.
Net billing tariff (NEM 3.0)
The current solar export credit rule for systems interconnected after April 15, 2023.
Parent-child exclusion (Prop 19)
A limited California property-tax transfer for a family home passed to a child who lives there.
Prop 13
California constitutional limit on property tax: 1% plus voter-approved bonds; the assessed value rises by at most 2% a year.
Prop 19
A 2020 California measure narrowing parent-child transfers to the family home, with a value limit and an occupancy condition.
Reinvestment risk
The risk that proceeds, invested later or at a different return, earn less than assumed.
Section 121 exclusion
Exclusion of up to $250,000 (single) or $500,000 (joint) of gain on a primary residence owned and used for 2 of the last 5 years.
Stylised crash test
A simple path with the depth and timing of a historical decline, used to test entry timing. It is not a daily replay.
Taxable value (Prop 19 base)
The value after an eligible transfer: the factored base value plus any market value above the allowance.
Unrecaptured section 1250 gain
Depreciation taken on rental property, taxed at up to 25% federally on sale.
