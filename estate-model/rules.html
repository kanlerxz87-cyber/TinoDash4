Decision criteria (fixed before model output)

Written 2026-11-25. Source: DECISION_RULES.md in the repository. Changes after model output must be dated and logged in COORDINATION.md.

  1. Primary metric: median real net position at the horizon, across the six scenarios and Monte Carlo draws.
  2. Downside guard: a path must keep its 10th-percentile real outcome within 10% of Path C's.
  3. Live (A, F): eligible only with a written commitment to occupy A as principal residence within 12 months. The same condition is applied to mixed path E (interpretation, flagged [F24]).
  4. Rent (B): eligible only with at least three verified comparables; until then it is a floor.
  5. Phased (D) beats lump sum (C): only if its worse black-swan outcome is at least C's and its base median is within 5% of C's.
  6. Mixed (E): chosen only if it beats the best pure path on the primary metric and the downside guard.
  7. Estate tax: no liability assumed unless the total taxable estate is verified above the basic exclusion.
  8. Ties: within 2%, prefer more liquidity at transfer, then lower regulatory exposure (interpretation [F25]).

What would make the household choose each path

  • A (live): written occupancy commitment; living value (including imputed rent) at least the sale median; 1-year Prop 19 filing achievable.
  • B (rent): verified comparables with a gross yield at or above the model break-even; a landlord policy quote; acceptance of full reassessment.
  • C (sell, lump sum): sale path leads on the primary metric and the downside guard is met without phasing.
  • D (sell, phased): passes Rule 5; household accepts 24 to 36 months of Treasury-level returns while waiting.
  • E or F (mixed): Rule 6 is met, or the household prefers a stated occupancy schedule.

Triggers that re-open the decision

  • Change to the Prop 19 indexed amount (next due 2/16/2027; BOE LTA 2025/009).
  • Change to the federal basic exclusion or to step-up in basis.
  • Verified change in Property A's assessed value or tax rate area.
  • Verified market rent for Property A.
  • Change in the household's occupancy plan.
