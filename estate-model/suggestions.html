Suggestions

  1. Obtain the verified base-year value for Property A from the County Assessor and enter it in the model (kept out of publication).
  2. Get a written date-of-death appraisal before any decision; the valuation sets the basis, the estate value, and the Prop 19 baseline.
  3. Ask an estate attorney to confirm the trust structure, the Prop 19 claim path (one-year occupancy and three-year claim), and any §1031 options before any sale.
  4. Ask a CPA to model depreciation recapture and the §121 exclusion for any part of Property A used as a rental.
  5. Collect three verified rental comparables and a landlord-policy quote before choosing path B.
  6. Set the liquidity reserve in months of household spending before proceeds arrive; the investing policy has a placeholder.
  7. Re-run the model yearly and after each change listed in DECISION_RULES.md §4.
  8. Keep Property B inputs and any household figures out of any public repository.
