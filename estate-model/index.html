What would change the answer

Under the current rules, the choice set has no occupancy path unless the household commits in writing to occupy Property A within 12 months (Rule 3). Rent is a floor until three verified comparables exist (Rule 4).

  • Written occupancy commitment. Without it, paths A, E and F are removed. With it, the pick for the 2040 transfer, 20-year case is D36 · Sell, park 36 mo, phase 12 mo, the same as without it. Living does not overtake sale in the base case.
  • Black-swan probability. The phased pick depends on the crash probability in the Monte Carlo draws: at 0% the lump sum (C) leads; at the 10% default, a phased path (D) leads (see what-if controls).
  • Rent yield. The gross rent yield needed for renting to match a sale (base case, 2040 transfer, 20 years) is about 6.3%, against an assumed 2.5% with no verified comparables [F11].
  • Living value. Living in A matches a sale only at a gross yield of about 8.9% (2040, 20 years, base case). Imputed rent is counted for occupancy paths only.
  • Base-year value. The Prop 19 base-year value is not yet verified [F08]. Until it is entered, the model reassesses Property A at full value, which is the conservative fallback.
  • Single-input tests. 1 of 8 tested inputs change the pick at least once (see what-if controls).

Default pick across all four cases (no commitment): 2040/10: D24 · 2040/20: D36 · 2050/10: D36 · 2050/20: D36.

Executive summary

  • Question. Live in, rent, sell, or mix Property A (inherited Santa Clara County home) and Property B (San Francisco home already owned), at transfers in 2040 and 2050 and horizons of 10 and 20 years.
  • Result under the rules. Without an occupancy commitment, the choice is among sale paths: lump sum (C) or phased (D). Phasing wins only if it passes Rule 5 (black-swan and base-case tests). Paths A, E, F are shown for reference.
  • Estate tax. Property A alone is well below the $15,000,000 federal basic exclusion for 2026 (Rev. Proc. 2025-32). No estate-tax liability is assumed because other estate assets are unknown (Rule 7). [F01]
  • Prop 19. Living in A preserves the lower base-year value only if the child occupies it as a principal residence within one year and files the claim within three years (BOE). [F07]
  • Black swans. Phased paths help in 1929-type and 2008-type crash timing but depend heavily on crash timing, which is an assumption. [F20]
  • Status. Model results depend on flagged inputs: rent, insurance, selling costs, capital-gain rates, and cash yield. See flagged for review.
