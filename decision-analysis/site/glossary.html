Glossary

TermMeaning
Base-year value (Prop 13)Assessed value set at purchase or transfer; grows at most 2% per year (inflation factor).
Change of ownershipA transfer that generally causes reassessment to market value.
Prop 192020 California measure. For a family home inherited by a child, keeps the parent's base only if the heir occupies it as principal residence; allowance added for excess value.
Prop 58Parent-child exclusion from reassessment; scope narrowed for non-family-home property (confirm).
Prop H (SF, 2026)Preliminary parcel tax on San Francisco property from Jul 1, 2027 (preliminary; legal text not read).
Documentary transfer tax (DTT)County tax on deeds; Santa Clara County $1.10 per $1,000.
Measure ESanta Clara County real property transfer tax on transfers in City of San Jose at or above threshold (city-only per county page).
Step-up in basis (§1014)Inherited property takes date-of-death fair market value as basis; gain before death is not taxed.
Basis consistency (§1014(f))Heir's basis must match the value reported on the estate tax return when one is filed.
Form 706Federal estate tax return; due 9 months after death.
Form 4768Application for extension to file Form 706 (6 months); does not extend payment.
Form 8971Basis information statement to the IRS and heirs; due 30 days after Form 706 due date.
Basic exclusion amountFederal estate and gift exclusion (2026: $15,000,000).
Annual gift exclusionAmount that can be given per person per year without using the exclusion (2026: $19,000).
Section 121 exclusionGain excluded when selling a principal residence owned and used 2 of the last 5 years ($250,000 single; $500,000 MFJ).
Depreciation recapture (§1250)Gain on rental real estate from depreciation taken, taxed at up to 25% federal.
NIIT3.8% Net Investment Income Tax above MAGI thresholds ($200,000 single; $250,000 MFJ).
Par yieldTreasury yield at par, the basis for the cash and park rates in the model.
T-bill / Treasury noteUS government debt; 3-month and 2-year par yields used here.
Real (inflation-adjusted) dollarsNominal dollars divided by the CPI index from 2026 (assumption for the long-run path).
Imputed rentValue of housing not paid for; counted only in the economic view, never in cash.
Monte CarloRepeated simulation with random market regimes (assumption-based).
RegimeA market state with its own inflation, growth and equity return assumptions.
Black-swan scenarioRare, high-impact event (e.g. 1929 or 2008) tested as a stylised path.
DrawdownPeak-to-trough fall in value of an investment.
Flagged itemA value that is blank, conflicting, unverified or preliminary. See Flagged for Review.
