Question

Which path for an inherited Cupertino-area residence (Property A, est. $3,100,000, owner estimate [flag]) is best for a household that already owns a San Francisco property (Property B)? Transfer year 2040, 2045 or 2050; horizons 5, 10, 20 years.

Base-case result (real $)

  • Top path (base, all combinations): Sell in the inheritance year; invest in equities; Monte Carlo top: Sell in the inheritance year; invest in equities.
  • Sell at 2040, 10-year horizon: $4,090,070; Live at same: $2,497,650 (no Prop 19 benefit; base blank).

Questions for counsel and CPA

  1. Is the decedent's estate below the $15,000,000 federal basic exclusion (2026)? Is Form 706 required? [flag F19]
  2. What is the decedent's Prop 13 base-year value, and does the heir qualify for the Prop 19 family-home rule? [flag F02, F03]
  3. Does the trust permit an heir to occupy, or must the property be sold or rented? [flag F04]
  4. Confirm the step-up basis and the §1014(f) consistency treatment for the date-of-death value. [IRS Pub 551]
  5. Confirm Cupertino is outside Measure E (San Jose only) and the transfer tax amounts. [flag F08]
  6. Confirm the recapture and NIIT treatment for any rental period. [flag F26]
