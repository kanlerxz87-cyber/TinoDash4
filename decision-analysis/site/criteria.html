Decision criteria

Criteria were written on 2026-11-25. They were written after test runs of a discarded draft model, and before the final figures in this build were generated. Changes must be logged as dated entries.

PathChoose it ifReject it ifDecision (dated)
Live
Live in the Cupertino house
  • The household will occupy the house as its principal residence within one year of inheritance (needed for the Prop 19 family-home rule).
  • The decedent's Prop 13 base-year value is documented and the Prop 19 benefit is material (verified value, not estimated).
  • Economic value of housing avoided (imputed rent) is judged worth more than the risk concentration of one house, after the household's other housing is counted.
  • Decedent's base-year value cannot be verified and the financial comparison without a Prop 19 benefit ranks below the best alternative.
  • The household will not occupy the house.
Model: ranks first in 0 of 9 combinations (base, 2026-11-25). Status: conditional (flags).
Rent
Rent out the Cupertino house
  • Rental cash flow after property tax, maintenance, insurance and water beats the best alternative on the financial measure over the chosen horizon.
  • Verified local rent comparables are at or above the HUD floor used in the model.
  • Property is reassessed to market value on transfer (the model assumes this when the heir does not occupy), and that cost is not recovered by rent.
  • Insurance for a landlord policy cannot be verified and the net yield falls below the T-bill yield.
Model: ranks first in 0 of 9 combinations (base, 2026-11-25). Status: conditional (flags).
Sell
Sell in the inheritance year and invest in a diversified equity portfolio
  • Equity return assumptions are met and the household can tolerate a 50% or greater drawdown after entry.
  • Stepped-up basis means little or no capital gain tax at sale, so the sale is near cost-free for tax.
  • The household has no plan to occupy the house.
  • The 1929 or 2008 drawdown test (see black-swan section) leaves the household below its required spending floor before recovery.
  • The household cannot tolerate the paper loss in the first 36 months.
Model: ranks first in 9 of 9 combinations (base, 2026-11-25). Status: conditional (flags).
Live→Sell
Live five years, then sell and invest
  • The household expects to live in the house for about five years, uses the §121 home-sale exclusion, and wants the property sale after that period.
  • Five years of occupancy beats renting or selling first on the financial measure.
  • Occupancy is not expected, or the house is needed for a different household plan.
Model: ranks first in 0 of 9 combinations (base, 2026-11-25). Status: conditional (flags).
Rent→Sell
Rent ten years, then sell and invest
  • Rent cash flow is positive after all costs and depreciation recapture (25% maximum federal rate on the recaptured portion).
  • Rent cash flow is negative after reassessment, or recapture tax exceeds the cash flow advantage.
Model: ranks first in 0 of 9 combinations (base, 2026-11-25). Status: conditional (flags).
Sell→Stage
Sell and stage: park proceeds in Treasuries for 30 months, then phase into equities over 12 months
  • The household is averse to the 1929-type or 2008-type entry risk (large drawdown right after entry).
  • The park yield (T-bills) stays close to the cash and bond return assumptions in the model.
  • Staging reduces expected return by more than the household accepts in exchange for lower drawdown risk, measured on the financial measure.
Model: ranks first in 0 of 9 combinations (base, 2026-11-25). Status: conditional (flags).
Live→Rent
Live five years, then rent out
  • The household expects to move out after five years and wants to keep the house as a rental afterward.
  • Post-occupancy reassessment or rent yield is below the alternatives.
Model: ranks first in 0 of 9 combinations (base, 2026-11-25). Status: conditional (flags).

Tie-breakers

  • If two paths are within the model's Monte Carlo 10th-90th percentile overlap for the same transfer year and horizon, the path with the smaller 10th-percentile (downside) outcome is not chosen.
  • Flagged or unverified inputs never select a path on their own: a recommendation that depends on a flagged input is labelled 'conditional'.
