HOW TO CLOSE THE GAPS

Private evidence-gathering sequence

  1. Keep deeds, trust, tax bills, leases, account records and prior returns in a private, access-controlled folder.
  2. Request assessor parcel history / tax bills and obtain independent appraisals, inspections, rent analyses, insurance quotes and five-year capital bids.
  3. Ask counsel and the CPA to evaluate every transfer and occupancy path, including a same-tax-year sale, Proposition 19, basis, depreciation, home-sale exclusions, local charges, estate portability, gifts and liquidity.
  4. Enter only rounded estimates and advisor-calculated after-tax amounts in the local calculator; run the same horizon and document assumptions.
  5. Recheck the laws and election/certification status from official sources. Record the decision and date; do not treat this site as a recommendation.
