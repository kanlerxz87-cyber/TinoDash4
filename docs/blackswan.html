Phased investing through 3M–2Y Treasuries (24–36 months)

Proposed structure: park proceeds in 3-month and 2-year Treasuries (live yields on 24 Nov 2026: 4.36% and 4.63% [S01]) for 30 months, then move into equities over 12 months. Park period range: 24–36 months. Phasing reduces the chance of entering just before a crash; it also gives up upside if markets rise during the wait. Reinvestment risk: the parked cash earns less if short yields fall (see the landing page).
