Mixed paths: definitions

  • Live 2 yr then sell: Prop 19 claimed; the §121 exclusion (up to $250,000 single / $500,000 if the surviving spouse rules apply) is applied to the gain at sale.
  • Live 5 yr then rent: Prop 19 claimed while occupied; then rent at market, which reassesses property tax to market value (Prop 19 does not protect rentals).
  • Rent 10 yr then sell: no Prop 19 base year benefit; rent taxed; depreciation recaptured at sale.
  • Sell now, phased: proceeds parked in 3M–2Y Treasuries for 30 months, then phased into stocks over 12 months.
