Property A: model treatment

  • Live path: base carried over only if the parent's base is known (blank here). Without it, the base equals the market value at transfer, so the Prop 19 benefit shows as zero (lower bound).
  • Rent or sell: reassessed to market value at transfer; basis for income tax is the date-of-death value (step-up).
  • Depreciation: 80% building share (assumption) over 27.5 years; unrecaptured §1250 gain taxed at up to 25% federal plus NIIT and CA ordinary rates.
  • Transfer costs at sale: agent commission 2.5% and title 0.5% (assumptions); county documentary transfer tax 0.11%; recording $92; Measure E 0 for Cupertino (confirm).
