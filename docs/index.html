Executive summary (as of 2026-11-25)

The model compares seven paths for Property A (Cupertino area, est. $3,100,000 in Nov 2026) over transfer years 2040, 2045 and 2050 and horizons of 5, 10 and 20 years. Property B (San Francisco) is already owned and is held constant across paths.

  • Base case, deterministic: Sell in the inheritance year; invest in equities ranks first in 9 of 9 transfer-year and horizon combinations.
  • Monte Carlo (1000 regime-switching draws, median): Sell in the inheritance year; invest in equities ranks first in 8 of 9 combinations.
  • Inheritance is not taxed at the California state level (secondary sources; flagged). Federal estate tax applies only above the $15,000,000 basic exclusion (2026, official), and the decedent's estate size is unknown (blank).
  • Prop 19 only helps a path where the heir occupies the house; the decedent's base-year value is blank, so the LIVE result is a lower bound.
  • Stress tests: 1929-type and 2008-type entry shocks change the staged path (M3) by a wide margin depending on timing. Staging reduces drawdown exposure during the staging window; it is not a guaranteed outcome.
  • Several headline facts are preliminary (Prop 3 and Prop 40 certification on Dec 11, 2026; SF Prop H and Prop I; Measure E city scope). See Flagged for Review.

Decision date: 2026-11-25. Re-run after any input change; the scenario report on the Dashboard states whether the recommendation moved.
