Inheritance decision: Cupertino inheritance vs San Francisco rental

What would change the answer (read first)

The ranking depends on which scenario you assume and on a few inputs. Under the base case, selling Property A and investing gives the highest total real wealth (10-year horizon, transfer 2045). In several stress tests the ranking changes, and in a long lost-decade test a rental path ranks first. The inputs that flip the top path are listed below.

Reinvestment risk (beside the headline)

Cash parked in short Treasuries earns 4.36% at the 3-month par yield on 24 Nov 2026 [S01]. If yields fall to 2.50%, each $1,000,000 parked earns about $18,600 a year less. The phased path rests on this rate for 30 months. Figures are approximate.
