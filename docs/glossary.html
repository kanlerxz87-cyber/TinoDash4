Glossary

TermMeaning
Base year value (FBYV)Assessed value used as the starting point for Prop 19 cap.
Prop 19 capFBYV plus $1,044,586 (current figure); taxable value cannot exceed this under the parent-child rule.
Step-up in basisReset of cost basis to market value at death. Current law; a cap is a proposal only.
§121 exclusionUp to $250,000 (single) or $500,000 (MFJ) of gain excluded on a home sold after 2 of the last 5 years owned and used as a residence.
Depreciation recaptureTax on depreciation taken on a rental (25% unrecaptured §1250 gain at max rate).
NIIT3.8% net investment income tax above income thresholds (not indexed).
MHSTCA Mental Health Services Tax, 1% above $1,000,000 taxable income (verify on Form 540 line).
Imputed rentValue of living in an owned home. Not cash; shown separately.
Real dollarsNominal dollars adjusted to 2026 purchasing power using the model CPI assumptions.
Reinvestment riskRisk that cash has to be reinvested at lower yields later.
3M–2Y TreasuriesU.S. Treasury bills and notes maturing in 3 months to 2 years, used as the parking place.
Monte CarloRepeated random simulation of stock returns to estimate a range of outcomes.
