Phased investing (Treasury bills, then equities)

Sale proceeds are parked in short Treasury bills (3-month to 2-year) for 24 or 36 months, then moved into a broad equity index in equal monthly tranches over 12 months. Parked cash earns the modelled cash rate [F15].

Sources: US Treasury yield curve (not fetched, rate flagged). Holding period: DECISION_RULES.md §1 (24 to 36 months).

Rule 5 check (phased vs lump sum)
